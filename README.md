# 📊 NETA - Executive Business Overview Dashboard

## 📋 Project Overview
This project is an executive-level Power BI dashboard built for **NETA**, a fictional software company, to analyze comprehensive business performance, revenue growth, customer acquisition, and subscription portfolio metrics[cite: 7, 8]. The dashboard is designed to provide C-level leadership with instant visibility into key performance indicators (KPIs) and actionable business insights[cite: 7].

## 🛠️ Key Metrics & KPI Highlights (Executive Summary)
* **Total Revenue:** $72M
* **Active Customers:** 400
* **Current MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue):** $1.7M
* **Average Revenue Per Customer (ARPU):** $11.1K
* **Churn Rate:** 11%
* **Renewal Rate:** 35%

## 📈 Analytical Breakdown & Features
1. **Revenue Trends & QoQ Growth:** Tracks quarterly total revenue alongside Quarter-over-Quarter growth percentages to identify seasonal patterns and business acceleration.
2. **Customer Behavior Analysis:** Monitors the growth of active customers versus newly acquired users over time, alongside quarterly ARPU trends.
3. **Product & Industry Performance:** Analyzes revenue distribution across different customer sectors (Technology, Healthcare, Manufacturing, etc.) and evaluates product lines segmented by discount tiers.
4. **Regional & Global Insights:** Breaks down revenue performance across global markets and regions (England, Bavaria, Provence, etc.).
5. **Interactive Filtering:** Includes comprehensive global slicers for Region, Industry, Product, Customer ID, Sales Rep, Status, and Subscription parameters.
